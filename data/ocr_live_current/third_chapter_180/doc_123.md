如图1， $ F\left(\frac{p}{2},0\right) $，设 $ P\left(\frac{y_0^2}{2p},y_0\right) $， $ M(x,y) $，则 $ \overrightarrow{PM}=\left(x-\frac{y_0^2}{2p},y-y_0\right) $， $ \overrightarrow{PF}=\left(\frac{p}{2}-\frac{y_0^2}{2p},-y_0\right) $，
